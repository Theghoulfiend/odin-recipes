Peanut Butter Energy Bars

Description

Protein packed energy balls can be made in about 10 minutes and they freeze well which can aid in meal prepping.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup peanut butter
  • 1/4 cup honey
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 1/4 cups old-fashioned or rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup shredded coconut
  • 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 1/4 tsp fine sea salt
  • 1/4 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 cup chocolate chips or other additions

Steps

  1. Combine peanut butter, honey, and vanilla. Stir.
  2. Add oats, coconut, flaxseed, salt, and cinnamon. Stir.
  3. Add chocolate chips or additions. Stir.
  4. Portion out the mixture via spoon and roll into balls
  5. Put in an airtight container and chill for 30-60 minutes
