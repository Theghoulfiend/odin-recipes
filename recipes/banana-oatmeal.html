Peanut Butter Banana Baked Oatmeal Bars

Description

Easy meal prep recipes that can be made quickly and has common ingredients that you may have laying around the kitchen.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cup of quick cooking oats
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 2 overripe bananas (mashed)
  • 1//4 cup creamy peanut butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 cup maple syrup
  • 1 large egg
  • 3/4 cup whole milk

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350F or 177C
  2. LGrease a 9x9in baking pan
  3. Mash bananas in a bowl
  4. Add peanut butter, vanilla, maple syrup, milk, and egg
  5. Stir until homogenous
  6. Add oats, baking powder, salt, and cinnamon and stir
  7. Bake for 25 minutes or until the top slightly spring back and the edges are light brown
  8. Let it cool
  9. Store in regridgerator, and then reheat in microwave or eat it cold
